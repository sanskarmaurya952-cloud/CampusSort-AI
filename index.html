THE FOUR CLASSES

Know where it belongs.

Follow local waste rules. Packaging and contamination can change the correct disposal route.

P

Plastic

Empty bottles and suitable plastic packaging. Rinse where required by local rules.

Pa

Paper

Clean, dry paper and cardboard are commonly recyclable. Keep greasy paper separate.

M

Metal

Empty cans and metal containers may be recyclable through local collection.

O

Organic

Fruit peels and suitable food scraps may go to a compost or wet-waste stream.
